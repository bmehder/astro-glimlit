A small editor makes the division between the page and its island easy to see. The article is static; the postcard below responds as you type.

## Messages give changes a name

Typing a title sends `UserChangedTitle`. Choosing a colour sends `UserSelectedTheme`. The model holds the current design, and the view presents it.

The browser still provides the familiar details of inputs and buttons, including focus and text selection.

Try editing your postcard here, then read on for saving and keyboard shortcuts.

## Saving is a separate action

**Save design** stores a snapshot in this browser. **Restore saved** brings it back. Editing alone changes the live preview without writing a new saved copy.

Enable keyboard shortcuts and press **N** while the demo is focused and outside an input to cycle through the colours.
